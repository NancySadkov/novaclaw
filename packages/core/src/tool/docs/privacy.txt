# Privacy
What stays on your machine and when information can leave.

NovaClaw works locally unless you choose a network feature.

## Local work

Chats, code, files, notes and the knowledge base stay on your machine. They are not
uploaded, not sampled, not used to train anything. If you run a local model, a
conversation can complete with the network unplugged — and there is an offline mode (an
airgap) that enforces exactly that rather than trusting you to unplug it.

If you choose a hosted model, that provider receives the requests you send to it.
Connected messengers and community peers also receive content when you use those features.

NovaClaw does not send crash reports or check for updates automatically. Local activity
logs remain on your device. Offline mode restricts network requests to local connections,
configured model providers and hosts you explicitly allow.

## Seeing it for yourself

The version and channel you are running are shown under Settings → About. Storage shows
where on disk this instance keeps its files, so you can look at them, back them up, or
delete them.
